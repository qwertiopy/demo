Controls

B pencil · E eraser · F fill · L line · R rectangle
O ellipse · I eyedropper · M selection · H pan

Drag to draw. Right-drag erases. Space-drag or middle-drag pans.
Scroll to zoom around the pointer. Shapes preview before release.

Selection: drag a box, then drag inside to move.
Ctrl/Cmd+C copies; Ctrl/Cmd+V pastes at the pointer.
Delete clears a selection. Escape cancels a gesture or selection.

Ctrl/Cmd+Z undo · Shift+Ctrl/Cmd+Z redo · Ctrl/Cmd+S save.
Save keeps a browser draft and updates Sandbox. Export downloads level.json.
