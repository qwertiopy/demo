Bindings

Every action can have up to two bindings. The same key or mouse button may also be used by multiple actions.

Click a binding slot, then press a keyboard key or mouse button. Bindings use KeyboardEvent.code, so they track physical key positions rather than typed characters.

Auto Fire repeatedly fires the currently selected weapon while held, respecting that weapon's cooldownMs. It is unbound by default.
